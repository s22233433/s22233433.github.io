What we manage

  • Screen creators for audience context, content fit, brand safety, and execution reliability.
  • Coordinate outreach, terms, product seeding, briefs, review, revisions, publishing, and live-link records.
  • Confirm content usage, brand reposting, paid-media use, and category exclusivity before production starts.

Who it fits

  • Brands with a defined product story, budget range, timing, and internal review owner.
  • Not a fit for list-only requests, guaranteed-virality expectations, or brands that cannot allow creator expression.
  • We do not accept group-buying, resale, or commission-led campaigns.

Platform, talent agency, or campaign partner

  • A platform fits teams that can run verification, follow-up, and reporting themselves.
  • A talent agency represents its roster; cross-roster comparison and coordination may be separate work.
  • Zhenguo works from the brand project outward, with decision records and execution checkpoints.

Prepare before outreach

  • Share product materials, prohibited claims, target market, channel, and launch date.
  • Set a budget range, seeding availability, and any known rights requirements.
  • Name the approver and acceptable revision rounds before the brief is sent.

Frequently asked questions

How do I choose an influencer marketing agency?

Look for a clear method for screening, rights, review, delays, and reporting, rather than a list of follower counts and fees alone.

Does Zhenguo guarantee reach or sales?

No. Results also depend on product, content, distribution, landing pages, and paid media conditions.

Can we ask for outreach only?

Possibly, but outreach without content rules, timeline, and delivery standards is difficult to manage responsibly.
