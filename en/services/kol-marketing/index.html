Decision Guide and Deliverables

When does this service fit?

KOL marketing is useful when a brand needs awareness, trust, and word of mouth. Zhenguo screens KOLs and KOCs by brand positioning, audience market, content style, and campaign objective, not follower count alone.

What will you receive?

Depending on scope, common deliverables include creator directions, recommendation rationale, collaboration terms and quote tracking, content priorities, review records, publishing links, and asset or usage-rights status.

What should be confirmed first?

Prepare product information, target market, budget range, launch timing, sample availability, and acceptable content and review rules. These conditions directly affect creator options and timing.
