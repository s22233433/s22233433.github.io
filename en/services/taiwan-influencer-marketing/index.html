When this service fits

  • Overseas brands with Taiwan distribution, a purchase page or a launch plan that need local unboxing, product experience or explanation.
  • Taiwan brands introducing a product and turning its features into understandable demonstrations and everyday content.
  • Brands and agencies with a strategy that need hands-on outreach, terms, review and publishing coordination. List-only buying and guaranteed-sales requests are outside this service.

Match channels and creators to the content job

  • Consider long-form YouTube for detailed testing and tutorials, and Instagram, Reels or TikTok for visual context and short content. Actual channel selection depends on the audience and recent creator work.
  • Review evidence of Taiwan audience fit, content compatibility, recent performance, brand safety, competitor restrictions and delivery reliability. Unverified audience data stays unconfirmed.
  • Use KOLs and KOCs according to the job, not a follower threshold alone. Each candidate needs a recommendation rationale and outstanding risks.

Work you can commission

  • Brief and shortlist: define audience, content job, channels, formats, candidate direction, selection rationale and risks.
  • Outreach and production: coordinate terms, quotes, timing, seeding, briefs, drafts and revisions.
  • Publishing and reporting: check live links, agreed content, asset lists, rights status and comparable reporting windows. The quote specifies which activities are included.

Milestones and brand decisions

  • Brief: the brand provides product details, Taiwan audience, budget and launch window; both sides agree scope and a review owner.
  • Shortlist: confirm candidates, fees, formats, rights and feasible dates before outreach and production.
  • Delivery: schedule receipt, product experience, draft, feedback and publishing. Discuss delays or replacements under the agreed terms.
  • Close-out: reconcile deliverables and open items, then hand over assets, links, rights and next-round recommendations. One viral post is not a campaign assessment.

Responsibilities for domestic and overseas brands

  • The brand owns accurate product information, Taiwan purchase or participation access, stock, shipping, returns and customer support. If these are pending, define launch readiness and feasible scope first.
  • Zhenguo manages the agreed creator work, content coordination and campaign execution. Importing, distribution, resale, customer service and regulatory review are not automatically included.
  • Overseas brands should confirm Taiwan sample delivery, usable product information, payment documentation and working language. Translation and extra production are separately agreed.

Fees and content rights

  • Separate creator compensation, campaign management, extra production, sample logistics and usage rights. A starting service fee is not the total campaign cost.
  • Agree original publishing, brand reposting, website use, editing and paid media separately by term, medium and territory. Publishing does not transfer every right.
  • Specify quantity, format, schedule and revisions before quoting. Unconfirmed extras remain estimates rather than a fixed market rate.

Prepare for an initial discussion

  • Product information, key benefits, restricted claims and a product or campaign page Taiwan consumers can use.
  • Audience, preferred channels, budget range, latest publish date, seeding method and one review owner.
  • Intended asset use and outcomes to observe, such as product understanding, asset delivery, traffic or qualified inquiries. List unresolved inputs for the initial discussion.

How delivery is accepted

  • Review agreed formats, quantities, live status, links, assets and rights. The cases below show existing content contexts and Zhenguo's role.
  • State data sources, observation windows and definitions. Views, impressions, reach and engagements are different metrics and should not be added together.
  • Reach and sales depend on product, content, distribution, pricing, channels and paid media. Fixed outcomes are not guaranteed.

Frequently asked questions

Can an overseas company commission Taiwan influencer campaigns?

Yes. Your company need not be in Taiwan. First confirm the Taiwan audience, product availability, budget, working language and review owner.

What if we do not yet have Taiwan distribution?

We can discuss audience, content goals and launch preparation. Resolve purchase, seeding and support limitations first; importing or distribution is not automatically included.

How do you choose Taiwan KOLs, KOCs and formats?

Match explanation depth, Taiwan audience and recent content, then check brand safety, terms and delivery feasibility rather than follower count alone.

Are creator fees and usage rights included?

The quote defines inclusion. Creator compensation, execution and extra rights are listed separately; do not assume a service fee includes all production, logistics or media.

Who handles seeding, review and translation?

Agree responsibilities before starting. The brand supplies accurate product facts and a reviewer; Zhenguo coordinates agreed seeding, content and publishing. Translation and extra production are confirmed separately.

What do we receive, and are sales guaranteed?

Agreed deliverables include candidate and terms records, live links, assets, rights status and close-out reporting. Reach or sales are not guaranteed.
