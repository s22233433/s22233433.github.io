Plan a Japan campaign

Japan project checklist

  • Provide product specifications, use instructions, accessible Japanese or English materials, and restricted claims.
  • Confirm seeding quantity, packaging, shipping timing, and the brand's review response owner.
  • Define format, publish date, revision scope, and category restrictions before outreach.

Brief and review

  • A brief should state essential information and fixed brand conditions without writing a rigid script for the creator.
  • Allow time for language confirmation, first draft, revisions, and pre-publication checks.
  • Balance product experience, everyday context, and commercial messaging around the creator's normal format.

Usage and paid media

  • Separate original creator publishing, brand social reposting, website use, and paid-media use.
  • Spark Ads, whitelisting, account authorization, and exclusivity should never be assumed to be included.
  • Write scope, term, territory, and asset formats into the agreement.

Common failures

  • Translating a Taiwan script without adapting product context or expression.
  • Allowing too little seeding or review time for a real product experience.
  • Waiting until after publication to clarify disclosure and usage rights.

Frequently asked questions

Do we need Japanese materials for a Japan campaign?

At minimum, supply understandable Japanese or English product materials, restricted claims, and use instructions. The working language depends on the creator and format.

How should revisions be managed?

Set approval milestones and an acceptable revision scope in the brief and terms so review does not become an open-ended loop.

Can Taiwan fees simply be converted to JPY?

No. Japan creator fees and rights must be confirmed locally; converted Taiwan figures are not Japan market pricing.
