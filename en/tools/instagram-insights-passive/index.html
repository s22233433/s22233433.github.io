PRIVACY & DATA BOUNDARY

Not a data broker.
Not account automation.

What it does Passively processes already loaded responses, page state, and visible DOM, then calculates and saves data locally.

What it does not do It does not read, store, or upload cookies, tokens, sessions, or passwords; it does not construct, call, or replay private APIs.

Storage Instagram, TikTok, and Threads data are stored separately in local databases and can be exported, imported, or cleared.
