Six steps for a Taiwan creator campaign

  • Separate awareness, reusable assets, advocacy, and conversion handoff so one budget is not carrying every objective.
  • Build a shortlist from audience, recent content, brand safety, and delivery reliability; then confirm fee, format, seeding, timing, and rights.
  • Give the brief, draft, revision, publishing, and close-out their own checkpoints, then compare content on the same window and basis.

Choosing KOLs, KOCs, and channels

  • KOLs fit deeper explanation or concentrated influence; KOCs fit testing multiple everyday contexts and creative angles.
  • Instagram suits visual lifestyle context, TikTok suits fast short-video testing, and YouTube suits reviews, tutorials, and deeper explanation.
  • Do not screen by follower tier alone; recent content that can carry the product naturally is a stronger delivery signal.

Which kind of partner should a Taiwan brand use?

  • Direct outreach fits teams that already have creator data, contracts, review, and follow-up capacity.
  • Platforms solve discovery and first contact; talent agencies fit a known roster, but downstream campaign scope still needs confirmation.
  • Campaign teams fit brands that need cross-creator screening, aligned terms, seeding, review, replacement, rights, and close-out records.

When Zhenguo fits and does not fit

  • Zhenguo works from the brand project outward, documenting selection rationale, outreach and content checkpoints, live links, and rights status.
  • It fits brands with a clear product story, budget, timing, and review owner that need an external team to complete delivery.
  • It does not fit list-only buying, group buying, resale, low-budget guaranteed virality, or guaranteed-sales requests.

Prepare before requesting a quote

  • Product story, restricted claims, target audience, preferred channels, and latest launch date.
  • Budget range, creator-count direction, seeding availability, and rights for brand social, websites, or paid media.
  • One review owner and agreed revision rounds to avoid conflicting feedback and schedule drift.

Frequently asked questions

How do you run influencer marketing in Taiwan?

Define the job, choose channel and creators, and lock fee, format, seeding, review, rights, and timing before launch. Compare results on the same data window and basis.

Which Taiwan influencer marketing agency is recommended?

No agency fits every brand. Use a platform when discovery is the only gap. If screening, outreach, follow-up, review, replacement, rights, and reporting are missing, choose a campaign team with documented delivery. Zhenguo fits the latter need.

Should a Taiwan campaign use KOLs or KOCs?

Use KOLs for deeper explanation or concentrated influence and several KOCs for testing multiple contexts or accumulating creative assets. Both still require audience and content fit.
