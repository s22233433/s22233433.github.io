Start with format, not platform labels

  • Instagram posts, Reels, Stories, TikTok short video, YouTube Shorts, and long-form video have different production and viewing dynamics.
  • For cross-posting, confirm whether the asset is the same, whether extra editing is needed, and whether each account needs separate authorization.
  • A creator's ability to present the product naturally often matters more than follower count alone.

KOLs, KOCs, and asset-only work

  • A focused KOL partnership fits deeper explanation or concentrated influence; batch KOC work fits multi-angle testing.
  • Asset-only production is not publishing, and paid use, editing, or website use must be agreed separately.
  • Separate awareness, asset, and conversion objectives so one budget is not carrying conflicting jobs.

Reading historical pricing

  • Public statistics need consistent market, platform, format, tax, and rights conditions.
  • When the sample or fields are incomplete, label it as limited observation or do not publish it rather than calling it 2026 market pricing.
  • A complete sample should state n, P25, median, P75, currency, and tax treatment.

Quote request checklist

  • Product story, platform, format, market, creator count, and launch timing.
  • Seeding, travel, appearance, scripting, review, revisions, and asset delivery.
  • Rights for brand social, website use, whitelisting, and paid media.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a fixed Taiwan influencer price in 2026?

No. Platform, format, production complexity, rights, tax, and management scope vary, so quotes must be compared on a common basis.

Why not publish individual historic creator fees?

Unpublished creator fees and payment data are sensitive, and inconsistent tax and rights fields make comparison unreliable.

How can we avoid overspending?

Lock the market, content objective, and rights scope before setting creator count; do not leave rights until after content is made.
