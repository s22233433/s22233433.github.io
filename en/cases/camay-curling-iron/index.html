CASE STUDY

Camay Hair Curler

Creator testing and feature breakdowns framed the product around men's styling needs, making the product benefits easier to understand.

Project Background

Camay needed a more tangible use case to explain the value of its curling iron in men's everyday styling.

Brand Objective

Help audiences understand root volume, bangs, and fast-heating benefits while reducing the barrier to trying a styling tool.

Market Challenge

Tool specifications are not inherently easy to feel, so the content needed to turn features into visible before-and-after differences.

Creator Selection Logic

A creator able to naturally demonstrate men's styling and explain the product through real testing was selected.

Content Strategy

Everyday grooming, step-by-step use, and key features were combined so the content kept a personal voice while explaining the product clearly.

Execution

Zhenguo aligned key messages, product information, asset checks, and publishing milestones to reduce the gap between technical claims and demonstrations.

Deliverables and Review

Deliverables included short-form and social assets. The review focused on which testing scenes could support later advertising and social reuse.
